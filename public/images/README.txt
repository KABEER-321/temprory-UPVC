CRITICAL OWNER PHOTOGRAPH DIRECTORY
=====================================
Per your strict project specifications, this website is exclusively engineered to use ONLY your original photographs.
No external stock imagery, no placeholder web pictures, and no AI-generated photographs are used.

To display your actual photographs on the website, simply copy your image files into this directory (`public/images/`) with the following filenames (or format of your choice, customizable in `src/config/images.js`):

1. `hero-building.jpg`
   -> Your original photograph of a completed residential or commercial building with installed UPVC windows.
   (Used for: Hero Section main visual and Project Gallery)

2. `installed-sliding-window.jpg`
   -> Your original photograph of an installed UPVC sliding window.
   (Used for: Products Card & Gallery)

3. `frames-ready-for-installation.jpg`
   -> Your original photograph of UPVC window and door frames ready for installation.
   (Used for: Products Card & Gallery)

4. `installation-work.jpg`
   -> Your original photograph of UPVC window installation work on site.
   (Used for: Services Section & Gallery)

5. `fabrication-machinery-workshop.jpg`
   -> Your original photograph of UPVC fabrication machinery and workshop.
   (Used for: Services, Workshop Section & Gallery)

6. `casement-window.jpg` (Optional / Additional)
   -> Your original photograph of casement windows.

7. `upvc-doors.jpg` (Optional / Additional)
   -> Your original photograph of UPVC doors and frames.

8. `completed-residential.jpg` & `completed-commercial.jpg` (Optional / Additional)
   -> Your original photographs of residential and commercial projects.

As soon as you copy these files here, the website will automatically display them crisply across all sections without needing any code changes!
